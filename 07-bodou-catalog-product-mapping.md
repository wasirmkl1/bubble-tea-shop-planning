# BODUO Catalog — Product Mapping Against Your Ingredient List

Source: `Product Catalog - BODOU_compressed.pdf` (uploaded to this repo), 64-page official BODUO Holding Group catalog. This maps every item on your requested list to the actual BODUO product name and case-pack ("Spec") found in the catalog, so you (and BODUO) are working from exact product names, not guesses.

**Note on case packs:** BODUO sells by carton/bag, not loose kg. Case packs generally run **~9–24 kg (or L) per carton**. This means "how much for 1 month" needs to be answered in **cartons**, not arbitrary kg figures — you can't order 1.5kg of a syrup, only whole cartons (e.g. 8 bottles × 2L = 16L per carton).

## ⚠️ Volume reality check

If you order **1 full carton of every matched item above** (~40 SKUs), you're looking at roughly:
- ~40 cartons × avg. ~15kg/carton ≈ **550–600 kg** total
- This is **far larger** than the ~107kg "first trial lot" sized earlier for ~1,500 cups/month.

**Recommendation:** Use the attached RFQ to get pricing/MOQ confirmation on your *full* wishlist (useful for future planning and negotiating leverage), but for the **actual first order**, consider cutting down to your top 4–5 syrup flavors, top 3 jam flavors, top 3 popping ball flavors, 1 creamer grade, and the core toppings (black tapioca, coconut jelly) — then scale up on repeat orders once you know what actually sells.
