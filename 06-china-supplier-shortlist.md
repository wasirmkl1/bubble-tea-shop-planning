# China Supplier Shortlist — Candidates to Contact & Sample-Test

**Important caveat before using this list:** these are storefronts surfaced via web search of Alibaba's public listings, matched against the first-batch items in `01-ingredients-list.md`. None of these have been personally vetted, ordered from, or verified for reliability, current pricing, or quality by us. Treat this as a **starting shortlist to message and request samples from — not a final recommendation**. Always run the vetting checklist below before placing a real order.

Source platform for all: [Alibaba.com](https://www.alibaba.com) ([Alibaba's own 2026 bubble tea sourcing guide](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/food-beverage/bubble-tea-ingredients-sourcing-guide-alibaba-b2b) confirms tapioca pearls remain the top-demand item, and highlights brown-sugar bursting boba as a fast-growing category).

## Vetting checklist before contacting/ordering (recap)
- Confirm **"Verified Supplier" / Trade Assurance** badge is present
- Check years active on platform and review count/recency
- Ask for a **paid sample batch** (~$20–50) before committing to the full first-batch order
- Prioritize suppliers who **specialize in bubble tea/boba** specifically, not general food traders
- Compare at least 2–3 suppliers per item before deciding — price and MOQ vary significantly even within this shortlist
