# Trimmed First-Order Shortlist — BODUO

This is a **cut-down alternative** to the full flavor wishlist in `07-bodou-catalog-product-mapping.md`. Same catalog product names/case-packs, but reduced to a realistic first-order size — useful if BODUO confirms (per the RFQ) that mixed-flavor cartons are **not** possible, meaning every flavor you pick locks you into one full single-flavor carton.

**Selection logic:** picked based on (a) flavors that appear across the most menu categories in your original list (so one SKU choice serves multiple drink types), (b) what's flagged as a proven differentiator in `05-competitor-landscape-bangladesh.md` (popping boba, brown sugar/cheese-style drinks), and (c) common bubble tea best-sellers (mango, strawberry, passion fruit, lychee, taro). **This is a starting suggestion, not a final decision** — swap flavors based on your own taste-testing and what samples you like best.

## What got cut (vs. the full wishlist) — and why
- **Dropped niche jam flavors** (Kiwi, Rose, Blueberry, Lychee, Raspberry) — kept the 3 most universally popular (Mango, Strawberry, Passion Fruit) that also double as syrup/popping-ball flavors, so your menu can lean on the same 3 fruit identities across drink formats for a more focused first menu.
- **Dropped ~11 syrup flavors** (Blue Curacao, Rose, Vanilla, Orange, Grape, Lemon, Kiwi, Peach, Watermelon, Blueberry, Pineapple) — kept 4 that overlap with your jam/popping-ball picks, so you're not buying and stocking a huge range of unused bottles.
- **Dropped 5 popping ball flavors** (Blueberry, Peach, Green Apple, Orange, Lychee) — kept the 3 flagged as best-sellers.
- **Dropped Grass Jelly, Brown Tapioca, White Boba, Lychee Jelly** — start with the single most essential topping (Black Tapioca) + one premium topping (Coconut Jelly) before expanding the topping range once you see demand.
- **Dropped Rose Jam** entirely (not a mainstream flavor for a first-time market).
