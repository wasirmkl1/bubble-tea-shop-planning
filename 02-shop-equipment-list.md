# Bubble Tea Shop — Full Equipment & Supplies List (A–Z), Takeaway-Only Setup

## 1. Core Beverage-Making Equipment
- Cup sealing machine (manual or electric — electric is faster, worth it once volume grows)
- Fructose/syrup dispenser (pump-style, for consistent sweetness dosing)
- Shaker machine (electric shaker saves huge time vs. hand-shaking every cup once you're busy — but hand shakers/cocktail shakers are fine to START with, cheapest option)
- Tapioca pearl cooker / induction cooktop + large pot (for cooking pearls fresh daily)
- Insulated pearl warmer/holding pot (keeps cooked pearls warm & fresh through the day)
- Commercial blender (for slush/smoothie-style drinks)
- Tea brewing urns/dispensers (insulated, keep-warm tea dispensers — brew big batches each morning)

## 2. Refrigeration & Storage
- Small commercial fridge/chiller (for milk, purees, fruit concentrates)
- Freezer (small, for ice storage or frozen fruit)
- Ice maker (or reliable daily ice delivery arrangement — common in Bangladesh, cheaper to start)
- Dry storage shelving (for tea, sugar, powders, packaging)
- Airtight containers (for tea leaves, powders — protects from humidity, a real issue in Khulna's climate)

## 3. Prep & Counter Equipment
- Stainless steel prep counter/workstation
- Digital weighing scale (for consistent recipes — critical for taste consistency)
- Measuring jugs/cups
- Strainers, tea bags/infusers
- Cutting boards & knives (for fresh fruit toppings)
- Timer (for pearl cooking consistency)

## 4. Water & Cleaning
- Water filter/purifier (RO system recommended)
- Sink with running water (required for food safety — check if your rented space has plumbing, or budget for installation)
- Trash bins (with lids, food waste vs general waste ideally separated)
- Cleaning supplies storage

## 5. Front Counter / Customer-Facing
- POS system or simple cash register (even a tablet + basic billing app works for a takeaway counter)
- Digital menu board or printed backlit menu board
- Order token/number system (simple numbered tickets — helps manage rush hour after school lets out)
- Small speaker for music (adds to the "vibe")

## 6. Electrical & Utility
- Voltage stabilizer (protects equipment from Bangladesh's occasional power fluctuations)
- Backup power (IPS/small generator — power cuts are common in Bangladesh; losing ice/milk to a cut fridge is a real risk to budget against)
- Adequate wiring/sockets for all equipment (get an electrician to check load capacity before installing multiple machines)

## 7. Interior / Ambience (Cozy, Fresh, Good Vibes)
- Paint (soft pastel tones — pick 2-3 brand colors)
- LED strip lighting / warm-toned bulbs
- Small plants (real or good-quality artificial)
- Branded signage board (exterior — critical for visibility from the street)
- Counter-front branding (menu decals, logo)
- Music/ambience speaker (already listed above)
- A couple of stools/small standing counter if space allows (even takeaway spots benefit from 2-3 spots for people waiting)

## 8. Staffing Essentials (even if family-run initially)
- Staff uniforms/aprons (simple branded aprons go a long way for "professional but cozy" feel)
- Staff hairnets/caps, gloves
- Staff training materials (your own recipe cheat-sheets, printed and laminated at the counter)

## 9. Safety & Compliance
- Fire extinguisher
- First-aid kit
- Visible trade license & food safety certificate display (builds trust, also often legally required)

---

## Lean "Day 1" list vs. "Growth Phase" additions

**Absolutely need Day 1 (minimum viable shop):**
Cup sealer, fructose dispenser, hand shaker, induction cooker + pot, small fridge, water filter, weighing scale, prep counter, sink, POS/register, signage, basic interior, fire extinguisher.

**Can add once profitable (Growth Phase):**
Electric shaker machine, ice maker, commercial blender, insulated tea urns, backup generator, expanded seating for the future dine-in transition.

This phased approach keeps your **initial equipment budget lean** while leaving a clear, deliberate list of what to upgrade first with reinvested profit.
